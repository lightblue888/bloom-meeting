# Using Bloom

Bloom is an AI assistant for group meetings. It listens to the discussion, answers when it is called, and puts the changes the host settles on into the paper.

## Before the meeting

After you open Bloom, go to Settings, pick a model source and enter an API key. Then go back to the lobby and drag the paper into the window. PDF, Word, Markdown and LaTeX all work. Importing takes a moment, and when it is done the lobby shows the paper's length and when it was last changed. Check that the microphone and the sound work, then click Start.

## During the meeting

Just talk as usual. Bloom turns what everyone says into text. When the host settles on a change, the AI puts it on the edit list, and once it is done the paper shows it as a tracked change.

## Inviting others

On the meeting page, click Invite, copy the link and send it.

## After the meeting

Once you click End, Bloom tidies up the meeting.
