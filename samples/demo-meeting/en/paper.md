# Using Bloom

Bloom is an AI assistant for group meetings. It listens to the discussion, answers when it is called, and puts the changes the host settles on into the paper.

## Before the meeting

Drag the paper into the window and click Start.

## During the meeting

Just talk as usual. Bloom turns what everyone says into text. The AI only answers when you say its name. When the host settles on a change, the AI puts it on the edit list, and once it is done the paper shows it as a tracked change.

## Inviting others

On the meeting page, click Invite, copy the link and send it. They open it in a browser, with nothing to install.

## After the meeting

Once you click End, Bloom tidies up the meeting. Under Review you find three things:

- Revisions: what changed in the paper
- Notes: what was discussed and decided
- Transcript: every line everyone said
